A thesis abstract is a brief and concise summary of the main elements of a research study. Its purpose is to provide readers with an overall understanding of the work, enabling them to quickly identify the research objective, methodology, key findings, and main conclusions. An effective abstract should accurately reflect the content of the thesis, highlight its principal contributions, and present information in a clear, objective, and understandable manner. It must be written as a single paragraph and should not include bibliographic citations, figures, tables, or information unrelated to the study. To ensure clarity and conciseness, the abstract should be limited to 200 words, allowing readers to gain a rapid and comprehensive overview of the research.
